Theoretical vs actual velocity (8 mm OD / 5 mm ID pipe)

v = Q / A, A = π·d²/4 = 19.63 mm² (Inner dia d = 5 mm, Outer dia = 8 mm, wall thickness = 1.5 mm). Re = ρ·v·d / µ (ρ = 1000 kg/m³, µ = 1×10⁻³ Pa·s). Flow velocity & Reynolds number are calculated using the internal bore diameter (5 mm). With Auto-record enabled, values are automatically captured when flow stabilizes near target Q (or click Record manually).
